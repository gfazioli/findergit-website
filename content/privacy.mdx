# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

The short version: this website sets no cookies and has no accounts, and the FinderGit app keeps your repositories, files and tokens on your Mac. Below is everything that does leave your device, and why.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is **{config.legal.owner}** ({config.legal.brand}), an independent software developer in Italy, VAT number {`IT${config.legal.vatNumber}`}. Write to [hello@undolog.com](mailto:hello@undolog.com) about anything on this page. See also the [Legal notice](/docs/legal).

## This website

- **No cookies, no tracking.** The site sets no cookies and does not follow you across other sites.
- **Visit statistics.** Visits are counted by privacy-friendly analytics that use no cookies and build no profile of you. What we see is aggregated: pages viewed, the referring site, country, and browser or device type. We use it to learn which pages help.
- **Server logs.** Like any website, the hosting provider that serves these pages processes your IP address and the details of each request (page, time, browser) to deliver the site and keep it secure, and keeps them for a limited time.
- **GitHub.** The sponsor pictures at the bottom of every page are loaded from GitHub, which therefore sees your IP address.
- **Your browser's local storage.** The site remembers, in your browser only, small choices such as having closed the newsletter prompt or the release banner. None of it is sent to us.
- **Email.** If you write to us, we use your address and message only to answer you, and keep them no longer than the conversation needs.

## The FinderGit app

- **Update checks.** The app asks this website whether a new version exists (on by default, in Settings → General). Each check is an ordinary web request: it carries your IP address and the app's version, and nothing about your repositories.
- **AI commit messages, default mode.** When you ask for a message, the staged diff is sent to this website, which passes it to an AI provider and returns the message. The diff is not logged or stored. Your IP address is kept only in the memory of the server that answered, to enforce the limit of 30 requests per hour, and is gone when that server is recycled. With your own API key, the diff goes straight from your Mac to the provider you chose and never reaches us. See [AI Commit Messages](/docs/ai-commit-messages).
- **Git hosting.** Pull requests, issues, stars and CI status are read directly from your Mac on GitHub, Codeberg, GitLab or Bitbucket, as described in [GitHub Integration](/docs/github-integration). We never see those requests.
- **The support window.** It opens at launch (Settings → General → *Show the support window at launch*) and also in About; it shows a sponsor's picture, which is loaded from GitHub.
- **Commit avatars.** With *Show author avatars* on (the default, in Settings → Detail View), the app looks up each commit author's picture on GitHub and, failing that, on Gravatar, which receives a hash of the author's email address. Turn it off to stop these requests.

## Services we link to

Downloads, from this site and from the app's updates, come from GitHub Releases, the newsletter lives on Substack, sponsorships go through GitHub Sponsors and donations through Stripe. If you use them, their own privacy policies apply. They share with us what they normally share with a publisher, for example a subscriber's email address or a donor's name and email, and we use it only for the newsletter or to say thank you.

## Why we may process it

- **Running and protecting the site and the app** (server logs, update checks, the AI rate limit): our legitimate interest in providing a working, secure service.
- **Understanding which pages help** (visit statistics): our legitimate interest, met without cookies or profiles.
- **Doing what you ask** (an AI commit message, an answer to your email): providing the service you requested.
