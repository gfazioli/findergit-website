## What FinderGit does, and what it cannot promise

FinderGit runs Git and other commands on your repositories and files when you ask it to, and some of them change or delete data: discarding changes, resetting a branch, deleting branches or tags (on the remote too), rebasing, pushing, pruning history in Maintenance, and Cleaning, which deletes the folders you tick for good rather than moving them to the Trash. The riskiest actions ask first and say what will be lost; others, such as a commit or a push, run as soon as you choose them, like the same command in a terminal.

Its suggestions are aids, not guarantees: an AI commit message is a draft to read before you commit, Repo Trust cannot make a repository safe, and the space that Cleaning and Maintenance say you can reclaim is an estimate. When you use AI commit messages, your staged changes are sent to an AI provider, as the [Privacy policy](/docs/privacy) explains; with your own key, that provider's terms apply.
