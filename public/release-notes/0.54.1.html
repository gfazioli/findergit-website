FinderGit 0.54.1

Release notes

FinderGit 0.54.1 — Storage counts right on a Mac with Time Machine

Bug fixes

  • The Storage card on Agents no longer reads "0 bytes" on a Mac with Time Machine. Time Machine's local snapshots keep the blocks of every file that was there when they were taken, and Storage had counted only what emptying the Trash frees at once. It now counts the space that comes back once the snapshots are removed, within a day, and says how much of it they still hold.
  • Copies made as clones, such as Chrome's code-sign clones, still count as freeing only what they really free.

Under the hood

  • The Agents page comes from AgentsKit 0.13.1, shared with Lancetta.
  • Translated into Italian, French, German and Spanish.

Install

  1. Download FinderGit-0.54.1.dmg
  2. Open the DMG and drag FinderGit into Applications
  3. Launch normally — the app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple

Requirements

macOS 15 or later. Universal binary (Apple Silicon and Intel).
