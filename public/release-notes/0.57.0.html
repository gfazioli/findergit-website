Release notes

FinderGit 0.57.0 — groups inside groups

Highlights

  • Groups in Root Folders can hold other groups. Right-click a group and choose New Group Inside… to start one, or Move to Group to put an existing group inside another, or back at the Top Level. A group lists its own groups first, then its roots, and every group remembers whether it is open or closed.
  • Move to Group names every group by its full path (Work › Clients), so two groups with the same name stay apart. A group can't be moved inside itself or one of its own groups, and the menu leaves those out.
  • Deleting a group keeps what it held. Its groups and roots move one level up, into the group it sat in, or out of any group when it was at the top.

Under the hood

  • Each group now records the group it sits in. Groups saved by earlier versions are read unchanged and become top-level groups, and a damaged list (a group whose parent is gone, or a loop) is repaired when it loads instead of hiding groups.
  • The tree's rules (order per level, moves, deletion, repair) are tested on their own, without touching your saved groups.

Install

  1. Download FinderGit-0.57.0.dmg
  2. Open the DMG and drag FinderGit into Applications (or open it from there, and it offers to move itself)
  3. Launch normally — the app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple

Requirements

macOS 15 or later. Universal binary (Apple Silicon and Intel).
