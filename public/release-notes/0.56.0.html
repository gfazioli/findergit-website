Release notes

FinderGit 0.56.0 — Rankings as charts, cards in order

Highlights

  • Rankings you can see at a glance. Largest on disk, Top starred (Overview), Top repositories and Languages (Account) open as a donut, as bars or as the list they were, with a switch in each panel. The donut sets every repository against the whole and folds the rest into Other; it is the default, and each panel remembers your choice. Hovering a name lights its slice, and a click on a repository opens it.
  • Cards in a better order. On the Overview the working tree comes first, then the disk, then GitHub, and each figure that opens follows the card it details: disk next to Largest on disk, stars next to Top starred. On Account, people, repositories, stars and activity are grouped the same way.
  • Organizations opens. Account's Organizations card now opens into a row per organization, with its name and login and a link to its page on GitHub, in place of the chips that were cut short at the bottom of Languages.
  • Stars opens like the others. The Stars card on Account opens into the repositories that recently gained stars, instead of a popover behind a + badge. The green + stays beside its chevron to say there is news, and opening the card marks it seen.

Under the hood

  • One shared chart view draws the four rankings, and lays itself out for a narrow panel at a large zoom: the legend moves under the donut and the bars stack their figures over the bar.
  • Translated into Italian, French, German and Spanish.

Install

  1. Download FinderGit-0.56.0.dmg
  2. Open the DMG and drag FinderGit into Applications
  3. Launch normally — the app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple

Requirements

macOS 15 or later. Universal binary (Apple Silicon and Intel).
