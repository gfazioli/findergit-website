FinderGit 0.52.0

Release notes

FinderGit 0.52.0 — Your GitHub profile on one card

Highlights

  • Profile Card. A new button at the top of the Account page opens your whole GitHub profile as one card made to be shared: ten figures, a year of contributions with your longest streak, best day and busiest weekday, your most starred repositories and your languages. Its tiles land one after another, as they do on the Account page.
  • Nine looks. Midnight, Aurora and Daylight; Upside Down, Hyperspace, Bridge and Nebula; and two that come with their season: Halloween through October, Christmas through December. Each sets its own colours, lettering and decorations, so the card is the same picture on any Mac, in light or dark mode.
  • Save, copy, share. Save the card as a PNG at twice its size or as a one-page PDF, copy it to the clipboard, or send it from the share menu. Backdrop sets it on a coloured ground with a margin and a shadow, ready for a post.
  • Public by design. Stars, forks, top repositories and languages count your public repositories only, so a private repository's name never reaches the picture. Pull requests and issues count those in public repositories.

Bug fixes

  • Saving the Overview as an image now draws it at twice its size on any display: on a non-Retina screen it came out at half the sharpness.

Under the hood

  • The card reads your public repositories from GitHub's GraphQL API a hundred at a time, and its pull-request and issue counts in a request of their own, so a failed count shows a dash and never blanks the card. The Account page's own query and cache are unchanged.
  • The Overview and the Profile Card now share one export path: render off screen, save, copy, share.
  • The seasonal looks follow the Gregorian calendar in your time zone whatever calendar your Mac shows, and an open card window moves on to the next day at midnight.

Install

  1. Download FinderGit-0.52.0.dmg
  2. Open the DMG and drag FinderGit into Applications
  3. Launch normally — the app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple

Requirements

macOS 15 or later. Universal binary (Apple Silicon and Intel).
