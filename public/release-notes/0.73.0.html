FinderGit 0.73.0

Release notes

FinderGit 0.73.0 — A Terminal family for the Profile Card

Highlights

  • Phosphor: your card on a 1970s–80s monitor. Green, Amber, White, or an 80s Color tube: the card glows on black glass behind scanlines, with a bezel and its power light, the curve of the glass at its edges and its glare. Above the card, finger and your login, the old command that tells who a user is; under it, a cursor that blinks a few times and then holds, and stays still when you ask macOS for less motion.
  • Printout: your card off a line printer. Green-bar, Yellow, White or Color paper, with its tractor holes cut through the picture, the ink struck in dots, a listing's heading with the date, and *** END OF LISTING ***.
  • Every letter monospaced. On a one-color look the avatar and your languages are drawn in the look's own ink, a language in the same shade in Top repositories and in Languages, and every small label still reads on the paper.
  • The glancecard window offers the same eight looks, which glancecard 0.7.0 draws for your READMEs. A Phosphor card reads the same on GitHub's light theme as on its dark one, so a card that follows the reader's theme is one file, and the window says so.

Under the hood

  • The looks are glancecard's, color for color: the same palettes, the same six language shades chosen to stay legible, the same paper and ribbon inks.
  • The classic looks draw exactly as before.

Install

  1. Download FinderGit-0.73.0.dmg
  2. Open the DMG and drag FinderGit into Applications (or open it from there, and it offers to move itself)
  3. Launch normally — the app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple

Requirements

macOS 15 or later. Universal binary (Apple Silicon and Intel).
