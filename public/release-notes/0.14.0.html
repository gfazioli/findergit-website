FinderGit 0.14.0

This release answers a question a disk-usage number can't — why a repository is so big — and makes the Overview lighter on the way.

🔍 Analyze history — find the real weight

Optimize and Deep Clean reclaim loose objects and stale packs. But a repository can be huge for a deeper reason: its history holds far more than your working copy needs — a stale branch left after a big merge, or a clone that pulled in a much larger upstream. A routine cleanup can't touch that.

The Maintenance tab's new Analyze (under History & bloat) diagnoses it — read-only, it never changes a thing:

  • A verdict on whether the history is bloated, with an estimate of how much is held beyond what your current branch needs.
  • The skew between your checked-out files and the commits the whole history carries — the tell-tale sign of foreign history riding along.
  • The heaviest branches, ranked, with the suspects flagged.
  • Up-front caveats (a rebase in progress, uncommitted changes, linked worktrees, a pinned pack) so you know what you're looking at before acting on it.

⚡ A lighter, faster Overview refresh

Refreshing the Overview on a big workspace is now smooth: it updates every repository in a steady, paced sweep instead of all at once, so it no longer overloads your Mac when you're watching many repositories.

🐛 Fixes

  • Opening a repository from an Overview list (Needs attention, Top starred, Largest on disk) now jumps straight to that repository the first time — before, the first jump from the dashboard could briefly show all your files.
