FinderGit 0.52.1

Release notes

FinderGit 0.52.1 — A calendar that fills the Account, and a graph on the Profile Card

Highlights

  • The Profile Card can draw your year as a graph. Beside the Style menu, pick the calendar or the graph: the graph is a line through your year, each day's seven-day average, in the look's own colour. The card keeps its size either way, and the image or PDF you save follows your choice.

Bug fixes

  • The Account's contribution calendar fills its panel, from edge to edge, however wide the window. On a wide window it sat against the right edge with half the panel empty, and its month names stood a few points left of their weeks.
  • The Account's contribution graph never dips below zero: its curve overshot under the axis around every quiet day.
  • VoiceOver names the graph as a chart in Italian and German.

Under the hood

  • The calendar's cells grow with the panel, never smaller than before, and scroll to the latest weeks when the window is too narrow for them. A test draws the real panel at three widths and checks where the grid starts and ends, and which weeks are in view.
  • The card's graph is drawn over the calendar laid out but hidden, so the two come out the same size, checked with and without contributions; its seven-day average is worked out once, by calendar day, in the card's figures.
  • AgentsKit 0.9.1, the same package Lancetta builds its Agents dashboard from; nothing changes on FinderGit's Agents page.

Install

  1. Download FinderGit-0.52.1.dmg
  2. Open the DMG and drag FinderGit into Applications
  3. Launch normally — the app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple

Requirements

macOS 15 or later. Universal binary (Apple Silicon and Intel).
